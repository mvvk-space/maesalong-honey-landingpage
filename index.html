Our Story

ผึ้งผึ้ง - The Blessed Bees

Nestled in the misty mountains of Doi Mae Salong in Chiang Rai province, our honey farm has been producing premium quality honey for generations.

Our bees forage among the wildflowers and tea plantations that cover the mountainsides, creating honey with unique floral notes that can only be found in this special region.

At ผึ้งผึ้ง which means " Honey Honey," we believe in sustainable beekeeping practices that respect nature and preserve the delicate ecosystem of Doi Mae Salong.
